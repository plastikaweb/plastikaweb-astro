# Lens: accessibility — WCAG 2.2 AA

You review the touched templates and their client scripts. The audience includes technical reviewers who will test with a keyboard and a screen reader.

## Already mechanical (input, not work)

ESLint jsx-a11y strict on Astro templates (alt text, labels, valid ARIA, interactive roles, no positive `tabindex`, no autofocus). The Playwright axe check (`e2e/`) on the pages it covers, both themes, 375 and 1440 px: contrast, names, roles, landmarks. Don't re-check what they report.

## Judgement checks

1. **Structure** (§2). Landmarks `header`, `nav`, `main`, `footer`; one `h1` per page; no skipped heading levels; decorative text (eyebrows, giant numbers, labels) never marked up as a heading.
2. **Actions vs. navigation.** `<button>` for actions, `<a href>` for navigation; no clickable `div`/`span`; the theme toggle is a `<button>` with `aria-pressed` or an equivalent state and a translated name.
3. **Accessible names in three locales.** Every name, `alt`, `aria-label` and visually-hidden text comes from the locale dictionary, never hardcoded in one language. Visible label contained in the accessible name (2.5.3).
4. **Language.** `<html lang>` matches the page locale; inline text in another language (language names in the selector, quotes) carries its own `lang`.
5. **Keyboard.** Everything reachable and operable in a logical order; visible focus (2.4.7) not hidden behind sticky headers (2.4.11); skip link to `main`; no keyboard trap in menus or dialogs; Escape closes them and focus returns to the trigger.
6. **Pages axe doesn't cover.** For a touched page without an e2e spec, check contrast pairs against `docs/design-state.md` yourself and say the spec is missing (tests lens owns adding it).
7. **Dynamic state.** Filters, form results and errors announced (`role="status"`, `aria-live`), not only painted; `aria-current="page"` on the active nav item and language.
8. **Forms** (§6.6). Real `<label>` per control; errors linked with `aria-describedby`; required conveyed; the honeypot hidden from assistive technology and the tab order; the consent checkbox not pre-checked.
9. **Motion and media.** Non-essential motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`; meaningful images have real alt text, decorative ones `alt=""`; targets at least 24×24 CSS px (2.5.8).
10. **Color alone.** Status, active filters and errors carry text or an icon, never color only.

Requirements §2, §4.4, §4.5, §6; `docs/design-state.md`; WCAG 2.2.
